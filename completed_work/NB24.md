# NB24 — Caption-service errors after download no longer abort the video

**Origin:** TODO recorded that transcript lookup handled disabled and missing transcripts, but every other caption-service failure propagated through download orchestration. After the media had downloaded, the worker therefore dead-lettered download-only work, or skipped the requested transcription fallback. [D16](../DESIGN.md#d16) accepted the fallback outcome.

NB24 was implemented together with [NB19](NB19.md) as one cohesive change, because NB24's error classification has to include NB19's timeout errors. Their shared decisions, validation, mutation evidence and review are recorded here. NB24 followed [NB18](NB18.md), which was committed separately in `88fe6bf`.

**Revalidation (`88fe6bf`, 2026-10-10):** source inspection confirmed the behaviour.

- `download_video_and_transcript_with_default_title` called `TranscriptDownloader.download_as_srt_file` with no exception handling.
- `YouTubeWorker.process_task` dead-lettered any `Exception` raised for the video.

Inspection of the installed `youtube-transcript-api==1.2.4` showed how its errors are raised:

- Service and playability failures derive from `YouTubeTranscriptApiException`.
- Transport failures surface as unwrapped `requests.RequestException`. `_raise_http_errors` wraps only `HTTPError`.
- Malformed caption XML surfaces as `defusedxml`/`ElementTree` parse errors, and malformed innertube data as `KeyError`, neither of which the library wraps.

`requests.RequestException` subclasses `OSError`.

## Decisions

- **Reporting (user, 2026-10-10):** an absorbed caption-service error is logged at WARNING with the URL and traceback. No dead letter is written, so `GET /dead-letters` keeps meaning "needs attention" and resubmission does not repeat completed work. This is recorded as [D39](../DESIGN.md#d39).
- **Timeout (user, 2026-10-10):** see [NB19](NB19.md) and [D38](../DESIGN.md#d38).

## Boundary

- **Shared group constraints:**
  - No live YouTube, Redis, Azure or Whisper access in tests.
  - Preserve the existing `TranscriptsDisabled`/`NoTranscriptFound` handling, the consent-before-network check and `publish_srt_text` publication.
  - Preserve D17 per-video dead-lettering for every uncovered failure.
  - Preserve the NB32 transcription-only contract and the `none` (download-only) meaning.
  - No new settings, wire schema or dependencies.
- **Non-goals:** B5 naming, scaling the YouTube worker, moving the per-video follow-up decision out of the worker (NB29), and slow-drip deadlines.
- **NB24 direction:**
  - Only the caption step in `download_video_and_transcript_with_default_title` catches `YouTubeTranscriptApiException` and `requests.RequestException`. It logs and returns `(video_path, False)`.
  - The video download stays outside the catch.
  - These still propagate:
    - unsupported-URL `ValueError`;
    - local publication `OSError` (the catch must not widen to `OSError`);
    - unwrapped parser errors;
    - programming errors;
    - `BaseException`.
- **NB24 acceptance:**
  - Listing and fetching service errors after a successful download preserve the media and return `False`, and a WARNING is logged.
  - `whisper` and `azure` tasks enqueue their normal follow-up with no dead letter; `none` completes with no follow-up and no dead letter.
  - Media-download, unsupported-URL, publication and programming failures still dead-letter only that video.

## Change

- **[downloaders/__init__.py](../youtube_whisperer/downloaders/__init__.py):** the caption step is wrapped in `except (YouTubeTranscriptApiException, RequestException)`. It calls `logger.warning("Caption service failed for %s; treating captions as missing", url, exc_info=True)` and sets `transcript_flag = False`.
- **[youtube_worker.py](../youtube_whisperer/workers/youtube_worker.py):** docstrings only; worker behaviour is unchanged.
- **[DESIGN](../DESIGN.md#playlist-videos-succeed-or-fail-independently):** documents the classification and limitations, marks D16 implemented, adds D38/D39, and corrects data-flow step 6.

## Review

- **Reviewer:** an independent Codex session, `gpt-6.1-sol` at high effort, thread `01a12828-ccc5-7100-bffa-1c562bcc8f51`. It is the same session that reviewed [NB18](NB18.md).
- **Assessment 1** (full Stage 2 target on `88fe6bf`, including DESIGN and integration with the NB18 parser): 1 Blocking finding.
  - **Finding:** the first candidate made `http_client` a required argument of the public `list_video_transcripts`. The one-argument call that works on `88fe6bf` therefore raised `TypeError`, contrary to [D18](../DESIGN.md#d18).
  - **Validated by the main agent.** D18 preserves public Python APIs regardless of internal call counts, and the boundary had wrongly treated the function as internal.
  - **Disposition:** fixed. `http_client` is optional and defaults to a fresh timeout session, and the compatibility test and two mutants listed above were added.
  - **Other checks:** the reviewer found nothing in timeout and session ownership, fallback classification, D17 behaviour or NB18 integration. It accepted all ten mutants, and its probes covered HTTP-error fallback, interrupt cleanup, borrowed-session ownership, existing-SRT consent, enqueue failure, and fallback before the parent acknowledgement.
- **Follow-up assessment:** the finding was resolved and no new findings were raised. In reviewer scratch, 184 focused tests and 22 probes passed (seed 1924), and the reviewer checked all twelve mutants.
- **Verdict:** no blocking findings.
- **Integrity:** HEAD, status and the candidate `git diff` hash matched each frozen snapshot after both assessments.

## Limitations and cleanup

- **Slow-drip responses:** a caption response that keeps trickling bytes is not bounded by the read timeout (D38).
- **Malformed payloads:** caption data that the library does not wrap, such as an XML `ParseError` or `KeyError`, still dead-letters the video.
- **Absorbed errors are visible only in worker logs**, by design under D39.
- **No live checks:** no live YouTube request was made.
- **Cleanup:** the implementer and reviewer scratch copies were deleted. The working record, review reports and full evidence logs lived in a temporary session scratchpad; this record keeps their essential contents.
