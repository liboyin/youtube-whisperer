# NB45 — Video and WAV rename their staged files

## Origin

After [NB39](NB39.md), `download_video` had yt-dlp write a fixed `video.mp4` inside the `StagedFile` directory, and `save_as_wav_file` had ffmpeg convert under the caller's destination name. Each then renamed its file to `staged.path`, which `StagedFile` names after the resolved target. The [D48](../DESIGN.md#d48) gap review (2026-10-23) proposed giving `StagedFile` a stage-file name option instead.

The user decided [D49](../DESIGN.md#d49) (4) on 2026-10-23: add it to pathlib-extensions and adopt it here after the push. The library commits are:
- `f0bc77c`: `StagedFile(path, policy, *, name=None)`, which rejects any name that isn't a single path component;
- `c679a0e`: deprecations of `OverwriteMode` and `publication`, which this project does not use.

Each was reviewed in the library, and the user pushed them; `git ls-remote` confirmed `c679a0e` as the remote `main` before this commit.

## Boundary

- **Intent:**
  - Video passes `name='video.mp4'` and WAV passes the caller's destination name, and both renames are removed.
  - The pin moves to `c679a0e`.
- **Preserved:**
  - Titles stay out of yt-dlp's output template.
  - ffmpeg infers the container from the caller's suffix even through a symlink whose target has another suffix.
  - The video regular-file check and its `FileNotFoundError`, and WAV's exclusive pre-creation.
  - Every NB39 completion-contract property.
- **Compatibility surface:** none. Signatures, results and messages are unchanged.
- **Non-goals:** other library adoption.

- **Callers:** [video_downloader.py](../youtube_whisperer/downloaders/video_downloader.py) and [waveform_loader.py](../youtube_whisperer/transcriber/waveform_loader.py) pass the stage-file name; the rename branches and the intermediate variables are gone.
